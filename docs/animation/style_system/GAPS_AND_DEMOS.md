# Future requests, known gaps and evaluation

This system reduces repeated discovery. A new action still requires original design, the selected asset and artistic review. No animation is produced by this milestone.

## Example: one sword attack

User: “Create a new sword attack in our Actions & Stuff-style motion language.”

Director route: Bible → Rig → Combat/Weapon → C01 source evidence → live manifest/selected weapon → existing authoring tools. If no sword is selected, establish that identity and its actual grip/reach before authoring; the current seven-item weapon manifest does not resolve an approved sword. Do not silently substitute a rifle or invent a source sword asset.

A useful **P** brief: grounded right-hand diagonal cut, compact load → short decisive arc → controlled guard recovery; whole rigid arm, receiving leg/body support, free-arm counterbalance. Propose hit timing only after blade reach and silhouette are visible. Source three-state sword sequencing informs contrast/recovery, while poses, curves, event windows and exact duration are original. Deliver one separate Pending Action for normal-speed Blender review. No Godot integration until authorized.

## Example: three-hit combo and player reaction

User: “Design a three-hit sword combo and a player hit reaction, then review the plan.”

Load C01 and C05. Verified third-person sword chain: `camimd` ground states `zefcct → thdqsk → eqlzfq`, minimum .3s repeat, air/riding exception. Verified player Hurt: `ismckx → gavocc`, distinct first-person `twrhbq`; retrigger guard .49s. These are reference facts, not our gameplay combo or stun rules. Propose original light/light/heavy contrast, separate hit/miss recoveries and a short receiving reaction; specify buffer/cancel/damage ownership as design choices. Do not claim zombie Hurt absence applies to the player. A plan-only request authorizes no Action creation.

## Example: ranged or enemy request

“Create an original shotgun fire cycle using this selected weapon”: load actual weapon geometry/markers, rifle handling principles and C06. Use approved research recoil as a motion reference, **not a commercially reusable whole Action**: its Aim base is source-derived. Do not inherit infinite-ammo rifle rules or add reload to the current rifle without a request.

“Add an enemy hit reaction”: select the actual mob/rig, inspect its graph. Zombie lacks a mapped skeletal Hurt in the traced route; Breeze/Warden have Hurt examples. Reuse existing runtime damage/death ownership and bounded impulses. Never rebuild native death physics because a skeletal Hurt reference is missing.

## Important remaining gaps

- Bedrock renderer/entity rotation semantics, negative animation weights, same-tick transition evaluation and engine query units lack a live VM parity trace. Godot's explicit approximations remain adapters.
- Three referenced vanilla player clips and the external primary geometry are unresolved here. Base-pack graph reachability is conservative; this audit does not certify every subpack/context or prove global absence.
- Foot-effect projection proves no per-foot load, contact duty factor or terrain IK. Re-audit real final sole contact on the selected rig.
- No verified equivalent is established for player spin, dash strike, custom hammer, timed parry/riposte or radial slam gameplay. Mace, spear and other-mob examples are scoped analogies.
- Selected sword/axe/spear/hammer production asset/grip contracts, combat root travel, hit volumes, buffering/cancel priorities and desired invulnerability remain future gameplay choices.
- Resource packs do not establish server damage authority. Source animation duration, cooldown progress, sound time and damage contact are different quantities.
- Sparse edited trailer shots cannot establish engine physics, rig topology or exact anticipation/contact timing; third-party footage playback calibration is unverified.
- Live Mobs scene registry, actual saved rig/slots and dynamic contacts require native inspection when authoring. Current Jump acceptance/manifest drift must be reported, not silently repaired.
- Commercial clearance and minimum-phone performance remain independent. The current desktop heavy running/fire result does not sustain the 110–120FPS target.
