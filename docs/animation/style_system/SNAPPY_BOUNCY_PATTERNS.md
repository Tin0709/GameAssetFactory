# Snappy, bouncy patterns

These are **P original patterns**. **S**=source verified; **O**=observed pixels; **A**=project adaptation; **P**=original proposal; **U**=unknown. Preserve rigid full limbs/unit scale. Continue from the [bible](MOTION_STYLE_BIBLE.md).

| Principle | Verified basis | Intended effect and artistic purpose | Rig adaptation; use / avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **16. Responsive anticipation** | **S:** ordinary source Jump begins from airborne queries; no normal grounded anticipation state is established. [P05](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md#p05) | A small preparatory cue communicates intention without making controls feel late. | **P:** use a restrained pose cue only when input/context permits; preserve immediate accepted launch. Avoid claiming a mandatory crouch is source behavior or imposing a fixed wait before takeoff. |
| **17. Arcs and compact exchange** | **S:** gait limb spacing accelerates/decelerates within a shared cycle. [P03](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md#p03) | A short ambiguous passing interval leads into a clear directional silhouette. | **P:** rotate whole legs/arms through connected arcs; use close tracks that clear the body. Avoid widening every pose, hiding passing with a jump cut or inventing knee folds. |
| **18. Small rebound, diminishing settle** | **S:** Landing mixes explicit Catmull keys with plain keys; source states also contain different fade-table shapes. [P06](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md#p06) | A modest return after absorption makes mass feel elastic yet controlled. | **P:** build load → small return → quiet recovery with target contacts preserved. Use the [approved impact control](APPROVED_REFERENCES.md); avoid repeated oscillation, a second midair hop or treating Catmull as automatic artistic quality. |
| **19. Overlap and follow-through** | **S:** gait, sprint posture, lifetime bob, head basis and transient Jump/Landing are ordered layers. [P01](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md#p01), [P05](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md#p05), [P07](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md#p07) | The impulse can lead while torso/arms finish their response and attention stays readable. | **P:** assign one final writer per channel and small purposeful delays. Use bounded secondary response; avoid independent full-amplitude waves, competing pose writers or double head compensation. |
| **20. Interrupt without losing intention** | **S:** source jump/contact branches, phase overrides and landing interruptions preserve context rather than requiring one full sequence to finish. [P05](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md#p05) | Starts, stops and reversals remain responsive while the displayed body stays coherent. | **P:** preserve current pose/velocity and release incompatible support before selecting the next placement. Use short reachable handoffs; avoid restarting at neutral, dragging stale anchors or waiting for a heavy landing to finish. |

Change **one** pattern; retain the [approved reference](APPROVED_REFERENCES.md), camera, travel and other layers. Review original speed and consecutive frames around maximum displacement/contact exchange. Test both leads, reversal, stopping during recovery and immediate repeat.

**U:** the source does not establish one ideal shoulder delay, universal bounce amplitude or dedicated turn animation for this target. Choose as **P**, then review with [timing](TIMING_AND_SPACING.md) and [weight](WEIGHT_AND_BODY_MECHANICS.md) checks.
