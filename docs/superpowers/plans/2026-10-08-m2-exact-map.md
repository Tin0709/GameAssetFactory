- Source minecraft_maps/source/Testing.litematic, actual volume 50 x 6 x 50; 5632 occupied /9368 air.

- Keep current Short Grass, existing study files, production Player/controller/camera/weapon behavior and Grassland map unchanged.
- No procedural scatter, placeholder final assets, new animation systems, individual plant physics or root-motion changes.
- 1 Minecraft block = 1 Godot world unit; one consistent schematic origin.
- No enemies at startup; preserve existing optional debug spawn controls where compatible.
- New versioned files only; project main scene remains Grassland for rollback. Run M2 scene explicitly.

## Review Focus
- Air holes and underground stone must survive face culling and collision.
- Upper tall cells must not produce duplicate plants.
- Imported mesh UV2/colors must provide planted roots to the existing wind shader.
- Logical matching alone cannot prove actual mesh transforms and collision height.
- Camera and enemy controls must work at the map's actual coordinate origin/elevations.
