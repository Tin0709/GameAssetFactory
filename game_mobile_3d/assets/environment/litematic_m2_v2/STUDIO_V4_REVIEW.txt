This pass responds to the approved Blender V3 preview and the supplied
Dungeons screenshots. It improves the current exact map's presentation;
it does not claim identical lighting or scenery to either reference.

Rendering changes:
- Retain the original approved V3 pixel shapes and native meshes.
- Build a runtime copy of the atlas, reducing only grass-top contrast to
  approximately 72% while preserving each tile's mean color and alpha.
  Include existing padding to preserve tiling. Source PNG files stay intact.
- Remove the previous extra green tint on vegetation and terrain.
- Warm key: rotation (-42,-45,0), energy 1.25, color (1,.96,.87).
- Cool fill: rotation (-35,115,0), energy .22, color (.90,.94,1).
- Ambient energy .12; AgX contrast 1.30; saturation .94.
- A faint camera-relative light affects actor render layer 2 only.
  Actors retain layer 1. Collision masks and gameplay logic are unchanged.
- Add lightweight voxel-neighbor corner occlusion to terrain vertex colors,
  with up to 24% darkening. Geometry and UVs are unchanged.
- Mobile renderer retained. MSAA 2x, 2048 directional shadow atlas,
  medium PCF filtering, blur 2, opacity .88, depth/normal bias .1/.45.
- Existing double-sided grass shadows and wind deformation are preserved.

Preservation and verification:
- 15000 logical source cells, 5448 exposed faces, 10896 terrain triangles.
- 2500 column collision rays and 4 outside-map checks: no mismatches.
- 512 short grass, 50 tall grass; native plant transforms/bend attributes match.
- Walk 4.25 m/s, sprint 6.25 m/s, stone collision, manual enemy spawning,
  all three weapon selections and wind interaction/recovery checked.
- Original atlas regions/native export hashes, source map, main scene,
  production player and R15 combat animation code preserved.
- Real GPU screenshots and shadow checks use the Mobile renderer on an
  RTX 4070 Ti SUPER in a hidden offscreen SubViewport. No desktop input.
- Flat-ground shadow test covers gameplay, overhead and isometric views.
- Phone performance has not been measured; shadow quality needs later
  device testing before selecting a final mobile quality preset.
