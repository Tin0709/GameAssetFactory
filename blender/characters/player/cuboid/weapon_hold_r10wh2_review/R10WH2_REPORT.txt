2026-10-08 | STUDY ONLY | Awaiting human visual review

BASE AND SCOPE
The approved R10-WH1 hold is A. B changes only UpperArm.R, ForeArm.R,
UpperArm.L and ForeArm.L rotation curves. The weapon placement, sight/eye
relationship, subtle head lean, torso breathing and scan curves are copied
unchanged. All original Actions, geometry, weights and bone rest data remain
preserved. No production or Godot migration is included.

ELBOW SHAPING
The elbow plane is lowered and drawn toward the body, so the upper segment
descends and the forearm rises toward its contact. This exposes an intentional
fold instead of letting the two cuboids read as one extended tube.

Rifle and shotgun contacts slide modestly back along the existing gun surface.
The rifle uses 6.5 cm rearward primary and 8 cm rearward support controls; the
shotgun uses 6 cm and 8 cm. These are internal hand-centre controls, not a gun
translation. A shallow lateral surface fit maintains the actual contact.

The pistol keeps its longitudinal contact positions. Its support forearm rolls
30 degrees around the gun direction to expose a corner touch and a stronger
upward fold. The already bent trigger arm retains a more open fold; forcing
both hands closer together would undo WH1's separated hand roles.

Representative ready frame 25, bend between upper-arm and forearm directions:
                         RIGHT primary        LEFT light support
Rifle / M4:              56.8 -> 69.5 deg      36.6 -> 70.1 deg
Shotgun:                 58.9 -> 79.7 deg      38.4 -> 54.2 deg
Pistol:                  77.6 -> 72.6 deg      39.2 -> 54.2 deg
Angle alone is not an aesthetic score. The pistol improvement comes chiefly
from the support fold and elbow plane, rather than increasing every angle.

CONSISTENT HOLD LANGUAGE
The CHARACTER'S RIGHT arm remains the primary control, with the gun on the
right-eye side (screen-left in a neutral front view). The LEFT arm crosses the
body and only lightly supports. Nominal signed vertex depth remains about
3 mm for the right contact and 0.6 mm for the left. The single WeaponCarrier
authority is retained; the left hand does not control a competing gun transform.

The original eye-face proxy and head lean are unchanged. Contact depth and eye
alignment are measurement proxies, not a finger grip or calibrated ADS camera.
Rigid cuboids still meet at their joints; this is a pose refinement, with no
rounding, bevel, rig stretching or anatomical mesh replacement.

LIVING BEHAVIOR
Ready breathing, moving hold, continuous look-around and turning retain the
approved timing. The left support gesture still follows the scan three frames
later (125 ms at 24 fps). The new elbow shape is baked throughout each clip,
with quaternion continuity and looping handles. There is no reset to a neutral
arm pose during the scan.

Native Walk remains 16 frames and Sprint 13 frames, at 24 fps. Both A and B use
the same original lower-body Actions, NLA timing and path/facing curves. Turning
keeps the original path-test case cuts; it is labelled as a reel, not a loop.
Sprint's 208-frame movie is an excerpt; its full combined loop is 832 frames.
