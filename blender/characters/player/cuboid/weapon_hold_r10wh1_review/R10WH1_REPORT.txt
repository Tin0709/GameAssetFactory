WHAT CHANGED
B is the new study; A is the actual saved R9-W4 result, using its unchanged
Actions. The weapon now sits on the CHARACTER'S RIGHT side: screen-left in the
neutral front view. The right elbow drops into a load-bearing silhouette and
the right terminal block contacts the grip/receiver. The left arm crosses the
chest for a shallow side touch. It does not own the weapon transform.

The single existing WeaponCarrier -> weapon mount -> weapon root authority is
retained. There is no competing left-hand constraint and no persistent IK.
The final animation is upper-body FK on the existing rigid-limb rig.

RIGHT-EYE ALIGNMENT AND HEAD LEAN
The main gun Base mesh's longitudinal sight line is aimed through a point on
the existing head's right-eye/front-face patch (Head local -0.1125, 0.275,
0.225 metres). The sight height and forward distance adapt per gun. This is an
explicit visual alignment proxy, not a calibrated ADS camera or an eye bone.
Neck and Head contribute approximately four degrees of lean toward the gun,
with small pitch/breath changes. The head/weapon relationship continues during
scanning instead of resetting to a centred carry.

For the new shotgun study, the socket is measured from the main Base mesh,
not the moving Pump component. This corrects its stock placement in B only.

LEFT LIGHT SUPPORT
The left terminal block touches the side/underside by an edge with a nominal
0.6 mm signed vertex depth; the right contact uses approximately 3 mm. These
are shallow contact proxies, not exact penetration volumes or finger grips.
The cuboid rig has no fingers: the softer role is conveyed by asymmetry,
terminal orientation, elbow position and follow motion. Judge that reading
in the front, three-quarter, side and rear previews.

Rifle and shotgun retain their distinct stock lengths and sight heights.
The pistol was revised after visual review: it moves 7 cm farther forward,
the right hand sits lower at the grip and the left touch sits higher at the
side. This separates the hand blocks that previously clustered below the face.
It remains closer than the long guns to respect the existing arm lengths.

LIVING MOTION
Ready has a four-second breathing cycle. Moving has a 64-frame upper-body
cycle. Look-around is a continuous 12-second left/right scan with breathing,
small torso response and uninterrupted eye/weapon alignment. The left support
gesture follows the scan by three frames (125 ms at 24 fps); contact is retained
while its elbow/pole and contact target respond more softly.

Turning uses the existing turn-direction response, with a delayed left gesture.
Original path-test case boundaries remain visible in the 19-second reel. Those
cuts are labelled in the review page and are not presented as a seamless loop.
The main turning previews use a closer camera that tracks the shared path
translation with a fixed view direction. Wide trajectory movies are retained
under each turning clip for context. This changes the camera only.
A narrow rifle contact interval initially caused a support-hand jump; a finer
contact search removes it. The obsolete diagnostic is explicitly named
rifle_motion_probe_before_fix.json. Current evidence is validation.json.

VERIFICATION AND REVIEW LIMITS
validation.json checks every upper study at half-frame intervals: right-eye
proxy alignment, right-side ownership, cross-body left support, shallow contact,
head/chest clearance, connected elbows, unit bone scale and loop continuity.
review_validation.json checks composed A/B scenes, native source digests,
representative locomotion/path frames and full combined Sprint loop closure.
media_validation.json records full video decode, frame count, fps and hashes.
file_preservation.json independently checks all 10,140 protected source files.
final_save.json records exact checkpoint-to-delivery-file byte verification.

These tests establish mechanical integrity. They do not certify a natural-looking
pose, a full physical grip or artistic approval. Review ownership, support
softness, head relationship and motion in the actual clips. Production migration
is intentionally stopped until human review of this study.
