The left arm now carries the weapon. Both arms extend forward with near-straight
elbows; shoulder-to-terminal-block centre reach is approximately 0.587 m from
the existing 0.600 m maximum. The right block hand hovers beside the receiver
instead of clamping it. Rifle support moved to the fore-end; pistol support
uses its grip underside; shotgun support uses its fore-end. Native weapon
geometry and existing scales are preserved. The existing WeaponCarrier remains
the sole weapon transform authority; the arms are coordinated baked FK.

Hold, moving hold and aim sweep share that design. Spine, Chest, Neck and Head
participate in breathing and facing response. The carrier and supporting arm
lead; the hovering-arm gesture follows by 125 ms. Turning previews drive the
response from the original signed facing-rate sequence, at 65% upper-gesture
amplitude to avoid rapid hovering-wrist roll on the fastest turn-rate ramps.
Actual world facing and all original locomotion remain unchanged.

Measured off-hand clearance (all public action and composed preview samples):
  M4A1/rifle: conservative lower bound >= 29.72 mm; upper bound <= 31.92 mm.
  Pistol:     conservative lower bound >= 28.79 mm; upper bound <= 41.47 mm.
  Shotgun:    conservative lower bound >= 28.25 mm; upper bound <= 57.92 mm.
These are triangle/box SAT separation lower bounds and nearest-vertex distance
upper bounds to the full gun, not exact minimum-distance measurements. Each
sample's true surface distance lies between its bounds. The upper bound can
be loose on broad flat faces. Whole right upper-arm and forearm/gun intersection
counts are zero. The carrying block remains within 1.74 mm of its support
surface. Contact at the primary arm is intentional. Deep Head/Chest intersection
tests use a 5 mm inset and found none; they are not a claim of perfect silhouette.

The mechanical language remains extended and separated during Walk, Sprint,
aim sweeps and turning. The rendered sequence keeps the same gait and path
phase in A and B. Native Walk is 16 frames, Sprint is 13 frames, both at 24 fps;
original path speeds remain 1.4 m/s and 2.1 m/s. Canonical V3 left/right turn
poses are mixed at the continuous native gait phase in private preview layers.

Remaining limitations and review points:
  The carry is high; the large off-hand block can overlap the face/receiver in
  some projections. Physical separation does not guarantee a clearly visible
  gap from every camera. The pistol is small relative to the existing fists.
  The shotgun stock projects behind the supporting side. These points should
  be judged in the side/rear boards and the moving comparisons.
  Fingerless rigid blocks limit support/trigger gesture detail. The new Ready
  endpoint intentionally changes the earlier hold direction; future production
  Draw/Holster transition compatibility has not been established.
  Sampled geometry and continuity checks establish mechanical integrity;
  'more alive and natural' remains an artistic judgment for human playback.
