Arm layout
Both upper/forearm pairs are collinear and raised almost horizontally, matching the additional front and underside views. Right arm angles inward and carries the weapon. Left arm is one straight diagonal reach that stops short of contact. Both arms remain separated by at least 58 mm in the sampled poses; the left hand also stays clear of the weapon, following the latest 'do not touching' instruction. There is no elbow solver, pole choreography, contact fitting, finger grip, arm stretch or torso compensation.

Weapon placement
Rifle and shotgun are anchored at the right hand end. Following the user's final placement corrections, rifle is offset 35 mm forward and 35 mm up; shotgun is offset 60 mm forward and 100 mm up to expose its receiver above the block hand. Their barrels point along the character/chest forward axis. To retain the raised crossbow-style arm pose while clearing the rear firearm stocks, the dev clones use uniform weapon-root scale 0.84 for rifle (previously 1.12), 0.70 for shotgun (previously 1.0). Existing meshes and production assets are unchanged. Exact final sampled clearances are recorded in validation.json. Pistol keeps scale 1.0 and uses a fixed higher grip attachment so its slide remains visible above the cuboid hand; the arm directions are identical to the long guns.

Head and motion
Head tilts 2 degrees toward the right aiming side. Ready has a 2 mm breathing rise. Looking uses a single smooth shared upper yaw, without arm resets, delayed support mechanics or counterrotation. Moving and sprinting reuse the existing lower-body gait Actions and their phases; turning retains the existing direction-test path. No Walk-to-Run substitution.
