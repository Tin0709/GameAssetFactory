2. Cause supported by inspection
W2's right upper-arm/elbow plane brings the stock's lower rear corner into the
arm even though its terminal block has a small grip-side surface gap. A good
internal target fit alone does not prevent stock/arm surface overlap.
The single authority remains WeaponCarrier -> constrained reference carrier
mount -> M4A1 root/mesh. Interpolation and gait composition are not the cause.
Two affected negative-drive lobes were checked separately (peaks near 191.5
and 244.5). The earlier opposite sweep had no inset stock contact and receives
no mirrored correction.

3. Smallest correction tested and retained
Created LongGunAimAround_LeftRight_V3 by copying V2. Tested right elbow-plane
orbits on the duplicate, first with a fixed terminal center, then with grip-side
surface refitting. Negative orbit worsened contact. Retained a maximum positive
6-degree orbit, weighted by a quintic ramp of the existing negative aim drive.
Smaller orbits left more of the reported overlap; larger orbits were unnecessary
for this limited improvement. The surface refit avoids embedding the grip block
while allowing up to 4.39 mm internal center shift. No weapon experiment needed.
Only eight quaternion component curves on UpperArm.R and ForeArm.R change.
Affected keys are localized to two lobes within frames 176–258, with smooth
Bezier entry/exit. Shoulder positions, limb lengths and elbow connectivity stay
fixed. Rifle, support-arm, Chest and head curves remain exact V2 copies.
