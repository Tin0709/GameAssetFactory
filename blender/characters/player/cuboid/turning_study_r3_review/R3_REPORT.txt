PHASE AND COMPOSITION CONTRACT
Each turn starts by evaluating its matching straight V2 Action. It is not based on a V1 turn with pasted channels. Full absolute poses crossfade at the same normalized phase, phi=((f-1)/period)%1. Phi 0 is right-leg reach / left arm forward, phi .5 is the opposite; these are the inherited authoring anchors, not independently measured source contacts.
Leg pitch/roll and root-path channels retain the V2 time course, so stride phase is not restarted or moved to Contact on turn entry. Endpoint pose and tangent continuity are retained; duplicate terminal samples are excluded from cycle playback. World translation and heading belong to separate PREVIEW parent Actions. Root remains identity within every gait.
Left is anatomical +X with rest forward -Y. Positive world-Z yaw turns left; clockwise is right/negative. Camera-facing quadrant does not change that sign.

TURN RESPONSE AND REBASE DETAILS
Walk added axial bank varies approximately 4.19–5.52 degrees across each stride, rather than V1's constant 5.3. Sprint varies 6.79–9.13 rather than constant 9.5. These are sums of authored local axial roll offsets, not calibrated source/world lean measurements. The stronger straight V2 pose motivated a slightly lower mean bank, particularly for Sprint. Walk is restrained; Sprint is more committed without an extreme bank.
Hips carry most of the bank and a small gait-weighted turn bias: about 6 mm lateral Walk / 10 mm Sprint, modulated by 12%. The shaped acceptance/rise profile repeats per step; it does not return to zero turn every step. Added Hips yaw is about .35/.65 degrees with small modulation. Base vertical and lateral stride curves remain the starting point.
Whole-body floor correction preserves the matching V2 minimum-leg-bottom clearance plus 1.5 mm at authoring samples. The correction ranges about -5 to +16 mm Walk and -11 to +24 mm Sprint. Consequently total hips bob is about 7.07 cm Walk / 13.18 cm Sprint versus straight V2's 5.23/11.01; turning has slightly more rise/fall, not an exact world-space copy of the base bob.
Leg rotations and local translations are unchanged from V2 within numerical evaluation tolerance. No knee, shortening, scale or deformation. The compact front-projected recovery survives, including V2's down/back root routing at the deepest rigid recovery.
Arms preserve V2 pitch and axial twist. Added gait-weighted balance roll stays below about .30 degrees Walk / .45 Sprint. Sprint's inside shoulder gets up to 4 mm extra outward translation near the forward peak (about 4.010 mm between keys). That clearance avoids the banked head corner contacting the raised arm. It does not replace the cross-body trajectory or produce airplane arms.
Spine and Chest add small yaw and roll responses with separately offset modulation. Hips, Spine and Chest do not share one turning extremum frame. Their existing V2 posture and counter-motion remain present.

HEAD / NECK
Turn counter-bank is deliberately restrained: Neck offsets 9% and Head 11% of the added axial bank, leaving about 80% inherited. Stronger independent leveling caused head/chest contact at the rigid cube seam; reducing it preserves clearance without moving or world-locking the head.
Additional local Neck+Head yaw is 1.2 degrees Walk / 1.8 Sprint, not a sustained sideways stare. Full-turn Head orientation relative to rest is about 3.98–5.20 degrees Walk / 6.45–8.41 Sprint. Head vertical excursion remains about 7.33 cm Walk / 13.35 cm Sprint. Natural positional bob is retained.

FOOT SLIDING — STILL A LIMITATION
Sliding is substantial on these tight in-place preview paths and must not be mistaken for planted feet. A/B evidence separates the common path/cadence mismatch from additional turning-pose effects.
Matched intervals use the same leg and time in A/B with both lowest leg corners below 3 cm. Horizontal world velocity of the lower-face center is a proxy, not a recovered contact or physical foot lock. Near-ground recovery sweeps can produce high percentile values.
Across all paths, matched median speed is approximately:
  Walk A 1.218 m/s, B 1.209 m/s.
  Sprint A 2.104 m/s, B 2.050 m/s.
Average B-minus-A speed is -0.004 m/s Walk and -0.034 Sprint. On circles, the 95th percentile absolute A/B speed difference is about .11 m/s Walk and .35–.36 Sprint. Some samples worsen even though the average does not. This indicates the turn-pose effect is smaller than the underlying mismatch; it does not demonstrate acceptable planting. No gameplay speed, root motion, IK or deformation was used to hide it. Full per-path statistics are in preview_validation.json.

REFERENCE / VISUAL FINDINGS AND LIMITS
Both locomotion originals (271 front / 421 rear frames) and the turning original (589 frames) were decoded and hash-verified. Reviewed Walk/Run source sheets, turning entry, circles, return sequences and full-frame context. The report uses observable body/limb continuity, relative head/torso presentation and gradual recovery; it makes no claim about the source implementation.
The V1/V2 front comparison shows deeper recovery foreshortening, a higher/more forward arm and stronger straight-gait posture in V2. Turning bank now breathes with the step rather than remaining constant. Walk's turn response is modest; Sprint contributes a more visible diagonal body pose. Sampled A/B circle views retain ongoing arm/leg rhythm while the heading changes. At gameplay size, the bank is clearer than fine shoulder differences.
Remaining mismatches: the reference still has a more gathered limb silhouette in some views, richer waist/shoulder presentation, and more convincing curve/ground coordination. V2 retains a long blocky recovery segment from side/elevated views. The head follows the bank more than an independently level head would because of the rigid neck seam. Straight V2's buoyant/airborne phases remain; the turn adds some vertical excursion.
Exact rigid-leg example: Sprint_TurnLeft_Reference_V2, phi 0, frame 1 / 0.000s, Side diagnostic; the recovering left leg projects long behind the body even though it gathers in front view. Phi .5 is the opposite-side equivalent. A knee plus segmented geometry could independently fold the lower silhouette; an elbow could permit tighter forearm wrap. Independent head leveling could require revised neck/joint clearance. The source topology is unknown. None is justified automatically by this first pass, and any rig redesign requires separate user approval. No rig changes were made.
Review limitations: the assistant inspected ordered rendered/source images and numerical traces, not continuously perceived real-time playback. The normal-speed movies are provided for human judgment of naturalness, entry/exit feel, multi-stride circles, hovering and whether this is artistically closer than V1. Source cameras, angles and trajectory are uncalibrated; our paths are not frame-matched reproductions.
