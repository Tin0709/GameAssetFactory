OBSERVATIONS VERSUS DESIGN
The original 102829 recording was located, SHA256 checked, and all 589 frames
decoded at 30 FPS. Both full-frame overview sheets, all three consecutive-frame
entry/return sheets, repeated-heading/circle sheets and additional original
full-frame samples were inspected. The useful entry/curve/recovery windows in
the supplied brief were covered. Inspection used extracted images, NOT observed
continuous playback. Technical checks do not constitute artistic approval.

Visible evidence: intermediate body orientations, continuing opposite arm/leg
progression, oblique waist/shoulder presentation, small relative head/torso
differences and return toward upright movement. Faster curves look more assertive.
The source does not establish its algorithm, exact banking angle, rig topology,
foot contacts, head-lead delay, input or camera trajectory. Some projected lean
comes from forward gait lean seen at changing headings. The exit also includes
deceleration, so it cannot identify a turn-only damping constant. No FOV or size
changes were reproduced as body scaling.

The implementation is an original approximation using the existing R1 gaits.
It is not presented as a verified Minecraft implementation.

TIMING AND COMPOSITION
Walk: preserved 16 intervals at 24 FPS = 0.666667 s, keys 1..17, unique loop 1..16.
Sprint: preserved 13 intervals at 24 FPS = 0.541667 s, keys 1..14, unique loop 1..13.
No 1.60 multiplier. Terminal endpoints remain in the Actions.

Common normalized phase: phi = ((frame - 1) / period) mod 1. Phase 0 is the R1
right-leg reach / left-arm-forward pose; phase 0.5 is the opposite progression.
These are pose anchors, not verified planted-contact events. Both turn variants
sample the corresponding R1 phase without retiming or restarting the stride.

Runtime-style contract for this study: interpolate absolute full poses between
straight and the selected turn variant at the same normalized phase. Locations
use linear interpolation and rotations use quaternion slerp. Complete poses are
never added together. A sign change passes through the base gait in the S test.
World translation and yaw belong solely to a separate PREVIEW path parent.
Root has zero motion; bounded local torso yaw does not repeat full path heading.

Left/right are anatomical/travel-relative: model forward is -Y, left is +X.
Positive yaw about world +Z turns left (CCW from above). Screen direction is not
used to choose the turn variant.

CHOSEN POSE VALUES — NOT SOURCE MEASUREMENTS
At full turn weight, nominal additional bank is 5.3 degrees Walk / 9.5 Sprint.
Hips supplies 4.8 / 9 degrees, with 0.3 at Spine and 0.2 at Chest. A small lateral
Hips offset is 8 / 12 mm. Hips height is adjusted to retain base leg-bottom
clearance, with a 1.5 mm margin. Local leg progression remains the R1 cycle.

Yaw toward the turn: Hips 0.5 / 1 degree; Spine+Chest 1.25 / 1.75 degrees; relative
Neck+Head allowance 2 / 3 degrees. Neck+Head counter only one degree of the bank,
so the head follows the body while staying modestly differentiated. Full-weight
head vertical excursion is about 5.70 cm Walk / 13.10 cm Sprint on either side.
There is no head translation cancellation, world locking or invented lag chain.

Arms retain their gait and original R1 shoulder translations; the small balance
rotation is 0.5 / 0.8 degrees. No additional shoulder translation was introduced.
No knees, elbows, limb scaling, deformation, rest or hierarchy changes were made.

During validation, distributing too much independent waist twist and leg
counter-lean caused overlap near the rigid hip seam. The delivered V1s place most
bank in Hips and keep relative waist/head changes smaller. No extra versions,
torso separation or rig upgrade were used to compensate.

A/B PATH REVIEW
A: original straight R1 pose with preview path and heading.
B: same path, camera, speed and phase, with the turning-pose blend.
Fixed rear-oblique world cameras remain stationary throughout each panel/reel.
The elevated gameplay camera also has fixed orientation; no camera orbit stands
in for actor turning. A/B share the same path Action for each gait.

The 19-second reel runs both gaits together at normal speed:
0..3 s: straight -> left bend -> straight.
3..6 s: straight -> right bend -> straight.
6..10 s: S curve, with smooth sign change through straight.
10..14.5 s: CCW circle across multiple strides, then straight recovery.
14.5..19 s: CW circle across multiple strides, then straight recovery.
Hard cuts reposition the actor between distinct test paths; they are labeled
test changes, not claimed seamless locomotion transitions. Global gait time
continues. Turn entry and exit within every path do not restart it.

Chosen preview speed is 1.4 m/s Walk and 2.1 m/s Sprint, not measured source speed.
Blend weight follows signed yaw rate, with quintic entry/exit easing and a
120-degree/s full-weight reference. The bends peak at half weight; circles at
full weight. High straight-line speed alone yields zero turn weight. Both
circles accumulate exactly +/-360 degrees while keeping the same local sign,
including rear/front-facing quadrants.

Sampled A/B images show a restrained Walk response and a more readable Sprint
bank, ongoing arm swings, and return to the base posture. Both sides were checked
independently. At gameplay scale the main Sprint silhouette change survives;
small head and waist differences are subtler. Pose sheets use one shared A/B
crop per timestamp for detail; use the uncropped movie for actual world travel.

VALIDATION AND LIMITATIONS
All four loops have zero endpoint curve-value error and seam-slope error below
4.5e-7. No scale or WeaponCarrier tracks, and no accumulated Root transform.
Pure left/right and 25%, 50%, 75% blends were checked every 1/8 frame, plus direct
left/right midpoint stress checks. Eighteen pose/blend cases passed. The moving
reel was checked every half frame (911 samples per A/B/gait, excluding path-cut
interpolation). Minimum moving-preview leg bottom is about 4.35 mm above ground.
Evaluated A/B poses match their expected shared-phase poses with zero recorded
error. Sixteen left/right/Walk/Sprint entry tests cover 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% phase;
phase error stays below 1e-10 and recovery returns to the corresponding base pose.

No hits remained in the conservative inset body-box checks: arms/chest/head,
head/chest, leg/leg and legs/chest. The boxes inset surfaces by 15 mm and exclude
the top 70 mm of the intentional rigid hip seam. This detects major penetration;
it is not proof that all adjoining surfaces have zero overlap. Sampled images
show no new gross shoulder/neck separation or excessive head twist.

Foot sliding remains visible against the grid, especially on tight curves. The
rigid R1 cycles were not authored with world-space planted feet. The near-ground
lower-face-center proxy has median horizontal speeds about 1.07/1.23 m/s for
Walk A/B and 1.67/1.89 m/s for Sprint A/B. This includes foot roll/recovery near
the ground and is not a precise contact solver or source-foot measurement. No
perfect planting claim is made. A production contact pass would be separate work.

Remaining reference mismatch: more regular waist response, straighter rigid limb
recovery, reduced arm wrap, and a deliberately restrained turn lean. Preview path
radii, speeds, smoothing and angles are chosen test values. Continuous motion
feel still requires the user's playback review. No limitation presently requires
approval for a rig change; the existing rig supports this bounded first study.
