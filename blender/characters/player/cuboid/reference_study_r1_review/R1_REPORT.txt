The takes are separate, not synchronized cameras. Walk is already buoyant with broad

CHOSEN IMPLEMENTATION — NOT MEASUREMENTS OF THE SOURCE
Both gaits are original full-body poses with opposite arm/leg coordination, lateral
weight shift, hip yaw/roll and opposing spine/chest response. Actual rest-bone axes
were inspected: limbs use local +X for forward pitch; axial local +Y points upward.
No arbitrary shared delay chain, random noise or weapon stabilization was reused.

Walk: local leg arc roughly +20/-40 degrees with a small recovery-shaping term;
arms about +41/-27 degrees, modest inward presentation and a mild forward trunk.
Sprint: local leg arc roughly +30/-58 degrees with stronger recovery shaping;
arms about +64/-38 degrees, stronger inward presentation and ~9 degrees mean combined
trunk pitch. It changes silhouette, recovery, trunk action and bob as well as cadence.
These are chosen artist values. Local rigid recovery lift peaks at 18 mm Walk and
38 mm Sprint. Forward shoulder clearance adds up to 22 mm outward and 12 mm forward.
Small local movements do not accumulate; Root remains stationary.

Head rotation retains part of the trunk motion, without head translation cancellation
or world locking. Final measured head vertical excursion is 4.47 cm Walk and 10.06 cm
Sprint; corresponding hips excursion is 4.39 and 9.70 cm. Head orientation from rest
stays around 0.66–1.02 degrees Walk and 1.59–2.26 degrees Sprint.

RIG LIMITATIONS AND REMAINING VISIBLE DIFFERENCES
Existing one-piece cuboid arms/legs are retained. There are no elbows, knees, limb
deformation or animated scale. Compact recovery is suggested by arc, foreshortening
and small rigid lift, so it remains straighter and less tucked than some source
silhouettes. The candidate's forward arm has less apparent wrap across the chest,
and its torso response is more regular and restrained. The source's clothing shapes
and perspective also affect its silhouette; these were not copied into our model.
The rig supports a useful first-pass approximation. No essential impossible pose
was established that would justify requesting a rig change at this stage.

Front/rear pose comparisons were inspected first, then the elevated gameplay scale.
The larger Sprint arm/recovery shapes remain readable; small waist details become
subtle at game scale. Overall rhythm and feel still require the user's continuous
playback review. No additional versions or animation pack were created.
